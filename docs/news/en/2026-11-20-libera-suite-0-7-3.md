# Libera Suite 0.7.3: checkboxes, a pageless view, and documents that look right

Libera Suite 0.7.3 is out. Since 0.7 it brings checkboxes to spreadsheets and a pageless view to Words, and above all a long list of fixes: documents made in Word, Excel and LibreOffice now look more like they do there, and keep more of themselves when you save them.

[Install Libera Suite](../main/install.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## New

- **Checkboxes in spreadsheet cells.** In Libera Tables, **Insert > Check box > Cell checkbox** puts a checkbox in the selected cells, the kind Excel uses for to-do lists. Right-click one and choose **Remove Checkbox** to take it out. They are kept when you save as `.xlsx`.
- **A pageless view in Words.** **View > Pageless** shows a document as one continuous sheet, without page breaks, for when you are writing rather than laying out.
- **Paste text only in Tables**, with Ctrl+Shift+V: the text arrives without the formatting it had where you copied it.
- **More picture formats.** HEIC, AVIF and WebP pictures, the formats of phone cameras and the web, now come through conversions and PDF export.
- **Keyboard shortcuts in the menus on Linux and Windows.** The menus now show Ctrl-N, Ctrl-O, Ctrl-S, Ctrl-Shift-S, Ctrl-P and Ctrl-W next to New, Open, Save, Save As, Print and Close.

## Documents that look right

- **Fonts you do not have.** A document set in a font that is not installed is drawn in the replacement the document itself names, as Word does, and a font is found whatever the case of its name.
- **Charts from Office 2016 and later.** Sunburst, treemap, waterfall and funnel charts look as they do in Office, with their colours and labels; a workbook with one opens with all its sheets; and a document with one can always be saved.
- **Word documents** keep their style names, their positional tabs (the ones headers use to centre a title or push a page number to the margin), their compatibility settings and their character borders when you save them. Picture bullets stay pictures, a superscript's underline sits under the raised text, and a picture in a two-column page stays beside its text.
- **Spreadsheets.** A large OpenDocument spreadsheet opens nearly three times faster, an `.ods` keeps its page margins, and a CSV value such as `0x1234` stays as written.
- **Pictures.** Thin lines in technical drawings, in SVG, WMF or EMF pictures, are drawn rather than lost, and a picture pasted from the clipboard, a screenshot for one, is inserted.
- **Many more fixes** come from Euro-Office, the project the editors come from, which Libera Suite now follows closely: among them a good number for OpenDocument files.

## On the Mac

Exporting to PDF, HTML, Markdown or EPUB, and printing, now use the JavaScript engine that comes with macOS. The pages come out the same; each export takes a little longer, under a second.

## Linux on arm64

**Linux on arm64 has a current version again.** It had stayed at 0.4.1; it now gets 0.7.3 like the other platforms.

## Security

The converter includes the fix for a flaw in Brotli, a compression library used when documents are read (CVE-2026-111497).
